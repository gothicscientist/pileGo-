# pileGo! UI captures: manifest

> **Kept in the repo:** only the nine captures the film uses (`scan-photos`, `scan-loading-1..3`,
> `scan-review-1`, `scan-review-form`, `scan-review-savebar`, `crosslist-dialog-6`, `out-live`). The
> full set of 106 listed below is regenerated by the capture script (see the README).

These are real renders of the repo's web app (apps/web, Vite + React). The network is mocked with
apps/web/e2e/fixtures.ts plus stateful route mocks in `pilego-launch/capture/ui.capture.ts`.
Light mode, 1440x900 viewport at deviceScaleFactor 2, so full-viewport PNGs are **2880x1800**. Crops are
element screenshots at 2x. Fonts and images were loaded and animations allowed to settle before each shot
(Playwright `animations: 'disabled'`, except the scanning frames, which keep the live animation).
Background cutouts were turned off (`pilego-cutouts=off`), so the scan's cover shows the plain photo.

Re-run: `cd apps/web && pnpm exec playwright test -c ../../pilego-launch/capture/capture.config.ts`
(add `CAPTURE_LIVE=1` for the live-site shot). Scan photos come from `capture/prepare-photos.py`.

**Hero garment:** seed item `a2`. For every shot after the scan, the mock turns it into what the scan
produced: "Rust satin bomber jacket", $30, size L, Y2K, Excellent, rust/brown, polyester/nylon lining,
style tags bomber/streetwear/y2k, the same description, rust-bomber photos. The price is $30 because
the review's price picker turns the AI's $38 guess into a Balanced tier of $30 (see notes). Brand and SKU
are shown as "Unbranded" and OUT-QQQA2.

## Real feature notes (what the UI actually shows)

- **Scan:** the app asks for three key photos (front, back, label) plus up to 12 photos with optional extras. Photos can come from "Use my phone" (a QR code hands the camera to a phone) or "Choose photos". When all three key photos are in, a 3-second countdown starts ("Scanning in 3…") and the scan runs by itself. "Wait, I'm adding more" stops it; "Scan with AI" or "Scan now" starts it by hand. "Fill it in myself" skips the AI. There is an optional "Add a note for the AI".
- **While scanning:** the progress lines are a fixed, timed script (one new line every 1.3 s). They do not report the AI's real progress. The cover shows a green sweep.
- **What the AI fills in:** title, brand, size, era, department, category and type, condition, flaws, colours, materials, style tags, flat measurements (marked "AI est." with an estimate note), description, and a price guide. The scan also returns keywords, but the form does not show them.
- **Price at review:** the review does not show the AI's price as is. It shows three tags (Likely to sell / Balanced / Less likely). With only the photo scan to go on, the guess is cut by 20% "because photo guesses run high". Here the AI said $38, so Balanced was $30 and the field was prefilled with 30. "Check prices after saving" points to real comps on the item's Price tab (Check again, sold comparables).
- **Saving:** "Save and cross-list" saves the draft and opens the cross-list sheet; "Save, scan the next one" resets for the next piece.
- **Cross-list methods:** eBay is "Automatic" (official API; works with the browser closed). Vinted is "One click" (the extension, in your logged-in tab). Depop, Poshmark, Mercari and Grailed are "Auto-post" (the extension fills the site's own form in your logged-in tab and posts it). Each card shows the fee and take-home pay. Each marketplace gets its own generated title, description and category, and you can edit the title, price and description per marketplace before sending. Depop has no title field. The sheet always reminds you to be logged in to the seller sites in the same browser. Without the extension, the extension marketplaces "wait in the queue".
- **After sending:** the toast says "Sent to N marketplaces". Listings appear as "Waiting" and jobs as "Listing … waiting" (eBay) or "waiting for the extension". Later statuses are "Going up", "Live" (with "View on eBay") and "Needs you". Live listings have "Relist", "End listing" and "Listing kit". Nothing in the UI says "listed instantly"; it is a queue.
- **Out there:** three groups: "Needs you" (failed or stuck jobs with "Try again" and "Dismiss"), "On the way" (queued jobs), and "Live". Days live at 30 or more (the profile's stale setting) turn red. "Relist N old listings" only counts stale listings on marketplaces that can relist: eBay through the API and Vinted through the extension. Depop, Poshmark, Mercari and Grailed have no relist, so the anorak's 66-day Grailed listing is not counted. Relisting queues relist jobs ("Relisting X of N" while it runs, then the toast "Relisting 3 listings"), and they show under On the way. "Check for sales" syncs eBay or asks the extension to check.
- **Sold:** one sale takes the piece down everywhere else (stated on the sign-in page; the backend queues delists). "Sold, waiting to ship" lists the bin location with a "Shipped" button.
- **Supported marketplaces:** eBay, Vinted, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari, Grailed. There are no others.

## Oddities and caveats

- **Mocked state, not live data:** the item states after sending (`item-listings-progress*`) and the On the way rows after relisting come from route mocks that copy what the database functions `queue_publish` and `queue_listing_job` would return. The UI rendering them is real.
- **Extension:** the browser extension is stubbed as "installed and linked" (sidebar "Extension linked") for the bomber cross-list and Out there shots. All other shots show "Get the extension". `crosslist-sheet-noext.png` shows the sheet without it.
- **The "Back" photo** is a mirrored copy of the front studio photo (there is no real back photo), so it looks like the front.
- **Vinted's blurb** says "using the same engine as the Bumpline relister", which names another product.
- **Relist button after a relist:** after relisting, "Relist 3 old listings" comes back straight away, because the listings stay old until the jobs run. That is real behaviour, but it could look like nothing happened.
- **Text cut off in the previews:** the description textareas in the cross-list preview crops cut off mid-line (they scroll), so the last visible line ("Pit to pit, approx…") is clipped.
- **Price picker:** the review's price tags hang slightly tilted (this is the design). The Balanced tag is raised and kraft-coloured.
- **Clipped crops:** the receipt crop clips the top of its tape strip (use `pile-summary.png` for the full card). `scan-review-savebar.png` was taken at the bottom of the page, where the bar is not over any content.
- **No errors:** none of the captures show an error toast or a broken image.
